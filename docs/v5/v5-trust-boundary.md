# WARSOL v5 Trust Boundary

## Safe To Use

- WARSOL company name and broad technical positioning from existing source ledger.
- Public DB contact phone/fax/website as already represented in the repo.
- Public patent/R&D signals as evidence of technology direction.
- Product family and application guide wording framed as inquiry preparation and possible application context.

## Pending Official Confirmation

- Official CI/logo ownership and final brand assets.
- Final approved company address.
- Official company email and CRM/API endpoint.
- Product grade names, TDS, SDS, specification values, and downloadable files.
- Certifications, customer names, awards, revenue, capacity, warranty, and commercial case studies.
- Legal/privacy wording for production operation.

## Rules Applied

- No fake customer logos.
- No fake certification claims.
- No fake product grade names.
- No fake TDS/SDS downloads.
- No fake email-send success.
- No exact performance values without test conditions and official documents.
- Public patents are used as R&D direction, not as proof of commercial certification.

## Current Backend Boundary

The contact page is a frontend inquiry-preparation tool. It generates a local summary and supports copy/print. It does not send email, create a CRM lead, or store personal data.
